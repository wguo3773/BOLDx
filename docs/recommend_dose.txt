Evaluate the next cohort dose from entered patient histories

Description:

     Apply the same dose-allocation rules used by the simulator to
     observed patient-level data. No true dose-toxicity probabilities
     are required.

Usage:

     recommend_dose(
       history = NULL,
       ndoses,
       current_dose = NULL,
       method = c("bold", "prediction", "tite", "combined", "boin", "tite_boin"),
       config = bold_config(),
       excluded = integer()
     )
     
Arguments:

 history: Data frame in enrollment order with dose, dlt and followup
          columns. dlt=1 means a DLT has been observed; dlt=0 means
          none observed to date. NA is permitted only before follow-up
          reaches the assessment window. followup is elapsed follow-up
          in months. Optional prediction is 0, 1 or NA; eligible and
          intervened are logical columns. If omitted, eligible is
          inferred from prediction availability and intervened is
          FALSE. Use NULL for no enrolled patients.

  ndoses: Number of ordered dose levels, from 2 to 10.

current_dose: Dose of the most recently enrolled cohort; required for
          nonempty history. For empty history, defaults to the
          configured start dose.

  method: Design to evaluate: bold, prediction, tite, combined, boin,
          or tite_boin. Default is Original BOLD.

  config: Design settings from bold_config().

excluded: Previously eliminated dose indices. Pass the previous
          result's excluded_doses to preserve irreversible elimination.

Details:

     Evaluation is supported at cohort boundaries of three patients.
     Original BOLD and prediction-assisted complete-follow-up BOLD wait
     until observed follow-up resolves. TITE methods use partial
     follow-up. Combined mode additionally waits for intervention
     patients to resolve.

     Without intervention, a DLT or completed non-DLT follow-up
     replaces any pending prediction contribution. After intervention,
     an observed DLT is a known failure; no observed DLT leaves
     untreated status latent even after full follow-up. An intervention
     must have a positive prediction and an eligible patient. Actual
     intervention decisions must be explicitly recorded.

     sensitivity and specificity weight available predictions. rho,
     eligibility probability, timing and accrual settings generate
     simulation histories; they do not replace observed patient records
     in this function. No unknown untreated outcomes should be entered.
     This interface implements the package's research protocol;
     application in a clinical trial requires protocol-specific
     statistical review and validation.

Value:

     A bold_recommendation list with action (start, escalate, stay,
     deescalate, wait or stop), next_dose (NA when waiting/stopped),
     reason, excluded_doses, dose_summary and patient_status. It is a
     next-cohort allocation result, not a final MTD selection.

Examples:

     h <- data.frame(dose=c(1,1,1), dlt=c(0,0,0), followup=c(3,3,3))
     recommend_dose(h, ndoses=4, current_dose=1, method="bold")
     
     hp <- transform(h, prediction=c(1L,0L,0L), eligible=TRUE,
                     intervened=c(TRUE,FALSE,FALSE))
     recommend_dose(hp, ndoses=4, current_dose=1, method="prediction")
