Summarise operating characteristics across random scenarios

Description:

     Compute the equally weighted mean and sample standard deviation of
     scenario-level estimates, each obtained from its simulated trial
     replicates.

Usage:

     summarise_scenarios(x, group_by = c("method", "mtd"))
     
Arguments:

       x: Scenario-level summary data, or a scenario simulation result.

group_by: Reporting-group columns, including method. Additional
          columns, such as delta, can be attached to x before
          summarising.

Details:

     The SD measures between-scenario dispersion, not a standard error.
     For 50 scenarios with 100 replicates each, the SD is calculated
     from the 50 scenario-level estimates, not from the 5,000
     individual trials. It includes both scenario heterogeneity and
     finite-replicate simulation noise. Group by MTD category, upper
     toxicity gap, and design settings where appropriate. A group with
     only one scenario has undefined SD, reported as NA. Above-MTD
     percentages are first patient-weighted within each scenario, then
     equally weighted across scenarios. Missing duration for
     complete-follow-up methods is retained as NA.

Value:

     A data frame of point-estimate means and matching *_sd columns,
     with n_scenarios and the minimum/maximum replicates per scenario.

Calculation:

     For J scenario-level estimates Q_j, the reported values are

     \overline{Q}=J^{-1}sum_{j=1}^{J} Q_j,\qquad
     SD(Q)=sqrt{\frac{sum_{j=1}^{J}(Q_j-\overline{Q})^2}{J-1}}.
     
Examples:

     panel <- simulate_scenarios(rbind(c(.1,.25,.45), c(.15,.25,.4)),
       mtd=c(2,2), method="prediction", nsim=5)
     panel$aggregate
