Flag intervention eligibility from external predictions

Description:

     Apply the modeled positive-prediction intervention policy. This
     function does not fit a classifier or perform an intervention.

Usage:

     plan_intervention(prediction, eligible = TRUE)
     
Arguments:

prediction: Vector of binary predictions (0/1); NA means unavailable.

eligible: Logical vector, or scalar recycled to all predictions.

Value:

     A data frame containing prediction, eligible, intervene (logical,
     NA if an eligible patient's prediction is unavailable), and
     status.

Examples:

     plan_intervention(c(1L,0L,NA_integer_),eligible=c(TRUE,TRUE,FALSE))
